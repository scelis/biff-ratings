About the Show

Biff! is a podcast on The Incomparable hosted by Guy English, John Moltz, and Dan Moren.

The Rating System

The rating system is miticulously crafted from Arrow Season 4. The three tiers are:

  • Biff — Biff is great. He was the ultimate henchman.
  • John Jr — John Jr is middling.
  • Alex — Alex is the worst. He was a clipboard-carrying weanie who was so boring that when he was killed not even Thea, his girlfriend, cared.

Honestly, it's confusing. You should probably ask Guy to explain it on the next episode.
